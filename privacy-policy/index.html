1. Introduction

Welcome to Barqat ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application (the "App") and website (the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information, including personal data and personally identifiable information (collectively, "Personal Information") through our App and Platform.

📌 Important Notice

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App or Platform.

6. Data Security

6.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your data:

  • 🔐 Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • 🚪 Access Controls: Role-based access to sensitive data
  • 🔑 Authentication: Strong password requirements and 2FA support
  • 🧱 Firewalls: Network firewalls and intrusion detection
  • 🔍 Audits: Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • 👥 Training: Employee data handling training

6.2 Mobile Security

  • App runs on secure device OS (Android/iOS)
  • Credentials stored securely in device keychain/keystore
  • Biometric authentication supported
  • Automatic logout after inactivity

⚠️ Security Disclaimer

No security system is 100% secure. While we implement industry-leading protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to protect your login credentials and report any suspicious activity.

7. Data Retention

7.1 Active Account Data

We retain data for:

  • Current Orders: Until order completion and dispute resolution (30 days)
  • Account Information: While account is active
  • Payment Information: While account is active (not stored long-term)
  • Communication: Until deletion request or account closure

7.2 Inactive Account Data

After account closure or 2 years of inactivity:

  • Account data deleted or anonymized
  • Order history retained for legal/tax purposes (7 years)
  • Personal information removed

7.3 Analytics and Crash Logs

  • Firebase Analytics: Auto-deleted after 90 days (Firebase default)
  • Crashlytics: Auto-deleted after 90 days
  • Can be manually deleted from Firebase Console

7.4 Backup Retention

Backup copies may be retained for disaster recovery up to 30 days.

8. Your Privacy Rights

8.1 Right to Access

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Download your data in machine-readable format
  • Know what data we have about you

8.2 Right to Correction

You have the right to:

  • Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Complete incomplete information
  • Update information in your account settings anytime

8.3 Right to Deletion

You have the right to request:

  • Deletion of your account and personal data
  • Removal from marketing communications
  • Note: Some data must be retained for legal/financial reasons

8.4 Opt-Out Options

You can:

  • Marketing Emails: Click unsubscribe link in emails
  • SMS: Reply STOP to text messages
  • Location: Disable in device settings
  • Notifications: Disable in app settings
  • Analytics: Opt-out in app settings (if available)

8.5 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your privacy rights:

  1. Send request to: barqatfmcg@gmail.com
  2. Include "Privacy Request" in subject line
  3. Specify which right you're exercising
  4. Provide sufficient information to identify you
  5. We'll respond within 30 days
