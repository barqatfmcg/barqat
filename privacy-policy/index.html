1. Introduction

App Identifier

App Name: Barqat

Platform: Android

Purpose: FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) order management and delivery platform

Intended Users

Barqat is designed for and intended to be used by:

  • Retailers - Small shop owners and retail establishments
  • Riders - Delivery personnel and logistics partners
  • Storekeepers - Shop managers and warehouse operators
  • Distributors (Admins) - FMCG distributors and administrative users

🔞 Not for Children

This app is NOT intended for children under 13 years old. If you are under 13, you should not use this app. Parents and guardians should monitor their children's device usage.

Welcome to Barqat ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our Android mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information, including personal data and personally identifiable information (collectively, "Personal Information") through our App.

📌 Important Notice

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App. This policy is specifically for the Barqat Android application.

Note for Pilot Testing: During our initial pilot phase, we use basic registration (Phone & Password). SMS OTP verification is currently disabled and will be implemented as an enhanced security feature after the pilot testing phase concludes.

We collect information necessary to provide our FMCG order management service. Here's exactly what data we collect and why:

  • Phone Number - For user identification and account access. (Note: Currently uses basic authentication; OTP verification is planned post-pilot phase)

6. Data Security

Protecting your data is our top priority. We implement multiple layers of security to keep your information safe from unauthorized access, modification, or loss.

6.1 Encryption & Technical Security

  • 🔒 Data in Transit - All data traveling between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
  • 🔐 Data at Rest - Sensitive data stored on servers is encrypted using industry-standard encryption
  • 🔑 Password Protection - Your login credentials are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • 🛡️ Firewalls - Network firewalls prevent unauthorized access to our systems

6.2 Access Controls

  • Role-based access control - employees only access data needed for their job
  • Multi-factor authentication for admin accounts
  • Activity logging to detect unauthorized access
  • Regular access reviews and audits

6.3 Mobile App Security

  • Your credentials are stored securely in your device's secure enclave
  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face) support for extra security
  • Automatic logout after inactivity
  • App is regularly tested for security vulnerabilities

6.4 Security Practices

  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Data breach response plan in place
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy
  • Compliance with industry security standards

⚠️ Security Disclaimer

No system is 100% secure. While we implement industry-leading protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your login credentials. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, change your password immediately and contact us at barqatfmcg@gmail.com.

6.5 Data Breach Notification

If we discover a security breach affecting your personal information:

  • We will investigate immediately
  • We will notify you via email and app notification
  • We will explain what happened and what you should do
  • We will work to prevent future incidents

7. Data Retention

7.1 Active Account Data

We retain data for:

  • Current Orders: Until order completion and dispute resolution (30 days)
  • Account Information: While account is active
  • Payment Information: While account is active (not stored long-term)
  • Communication: Until deletion request or account closure

7.2 Inactive Account Data

After account closure or 2 years of inactivity:

  • Account data deleted or anonymized
  • Order history retained for legal/tax purposes (7 years)
  • Personal information removed

7.3 Analytics and Crash Logs

  • Firebase Analytics: Auto-deleted after 90 days (Firebase default)
  • Crashlytics: Auto-deleted after 90 days
  • Can be manually deleted from Firebase Console

7.4 Backup Retention

Backup copies may be retained for disaster recovery up to 30 days.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Data Control

You have rights regarding your personal data. We are committed to helping you exercise these rights in a transparent and timely manner.

8.1 Right to Access Your Data

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of all personal data we have about you
  • Download your data in a machine-readable format (CSV, JSON, etc.)
  • Know exactly what information we're storing
  • View your account information in the app anytime

8.2 Right to Correct Your Data

You have the right to:

  • Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Complete incomplete information about you
  • Edit your profile, contact info, and preferences in the app
  • Request correction of information via email

8.3 Right to Delete Your Data

You have the right to request:

  • Deletion of your account and associated personal data
  • Removal of your profile and account information
  • Note: Some data like order history may be retained for legal/financial/tax compliance purposes (7 years)
  • Once deleted, we will stop collecting new data from you

8.4 Right to Object & Opt-Out

You can opt-out of:

  • 📧 Marketing Emails - Click the unsubscribe link in any email
  • 📱 SMS Messages - Reply STOP to any text message
  • 📍 Location Tracking - When location features are enabled in the future, you can disable them in device settings
  • 🔔 Push Notifications - Disable in app settings
  • 📊 Analytics - Disable in app settings (if available)

8.5 How to Exercise Your Rights

To request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights:

  1. Email: barqatfmcg@gmail.com
  2. Subject Line: "Privacy Request [Type of Request]"
  3. Include: Your full name, email, phone number, and account details
  4. Specify: Which right you're exercising (access, correction, deletion, etc.)
  5. Timeline: We will respond within 30 days

8.6 Right to Data Portability

We will provide your data in a structured, commonly-used, machine-readable format so you can transfer it to another service if you choose.

10. Children's Privacy

10.1 Age Restrictions - Not for Children Under 13

The Barqat Android app is NOT intended for children under 13 years old.

This app is designed exclusively for business users including retailers, riders, storekeepers, and distributors. The app includes order processing, financial transactions, and location tracking that are inappropriate and unsafe for children.

10.2 No Intentional Collection from Children

We do NOT:

  • Intentionally collect data from children under 13
  • Target children in our marketing or app design
  • Allow registration for users under 13
  • Use child-directed marketing tactics

10.3 What Happens If We Discover a Child User

If we discover a user under 13 is using the Barqat app:

  • We will immediately disable their account
  • We will delete all associated personal data
  • We will notify parents/guardians at their email address
  • We will not reinstall the app for this user

10.4 Parental Rights & Safeguards

Parents and guardians can:

  • Monitor their child's device for app installation
  • Use device parental controls to restrict app access
  • Request account deletion and data removal
  • Contact us about any child privacy concerns

10.5 Report Child Users

If you're a parent/guardian of a child using this app, or if you see a child using it:

  • Contact us immediately at barqatfmcg@gmail.com
  • Include the child's name, age, and account information
  • We will take immediate action to remove the account
