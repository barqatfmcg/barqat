Retailer Challenges

What Dukaandaars Deal With Today

Running a neighbourhood store shouldn't mean endless friction, high purchase costs, and missed sales opportunities.

Multiple suppliers, multiple calls

Ordering from many salesmen and distributors means constant calls, missed visits, and stock-outs.

Higher purchase cost

Small shops buy in small quantities and pay more per unit than large chains do.

Order-booker leakage

Manual order-taking leads to wrong quantities, missed items, and disputes.

No visibility

No clear record of what was ordered, what was delivered, and what you owe.

Limited reach

Your customers are your walk-ins only. Shoppers who order from home go elsewhere.

Shop looks and merchandising

Few retailers get help presenting their shop the way modern stores do.
